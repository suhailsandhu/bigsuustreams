# Brag Plan: Courtside Stream

## What is this app?
Courtside Stream is an ad-free, high-definition, mobile-first basketball live streaming Progressive Web App (PWA) that bypasses shady popunder ad networks to deliver pure NBA & NCAA action via low-latency adaptive HLS streams.

## The angle
Free sports streaming is plagued by 5 layers of malware popups, fake download buttons, and laggy embeds. Courtside Stream reverses the video CDN pipeline to deliver the stream directly to an ultra-slick, dark-mode web player with zero ads, real-time NBA scores, and 4K capability.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, high-contrast confrontational callout:
"TIRED OF 5 POPUNDER ADS JUST TO WATCH BASKETBALL?"
Cross out intrusive mock ad overlays with a fast, heavy neon red slash: "ZERO POPUNDERS. ZERO REDIRECTS."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Live Matchup Hub Reveal**: Sleek dark card featuring Golden State Warriors vs. Los Angeles Lakers, active tipoff time, live pulsing badge, and real-time viewer count ticker (14,820 live).
- **Direct CDN Stream Pipeline**: Visual breakdown of the tech stack: Direct HLS bypassing ad trackers, instant 1080p FHD & 4K adaptive bitrate switching.
- **Mobile-First PWA**: Theater mode layout, zero lag buffering, installable directly to iOS & Android home screens.

## Outro / punchline
"WATCH BASKETBALL THE WAY IT WAS MEANT TO BE."
Logo lockup with Courtside flame-orange badge, courtside-stream live, and tagline: "100% Hoops. Zero Ads."

## User flow worth showing
1. Landing on the live basketball schedule hub.
2. Clicking a live NBA marquee game (LAL vs GSW).
3. Instantaneous, pristine HLS video playback with zero popups or redirections.
